            When Sweepwise ticks a suggestion for you and you untick it before approving, it remembers. Once: it starts unticked. Twice: it is hidden. Forget it to have it suggested again.

                Email

                Clears old newsletters and promotions out of your inbox. Sweepwise reads only the top lines of each email (sender, subject, date and the like), never what your emails say. It skips people you wrote to in the past year and mail that looks like bank, government or health mail, security alerts or receipts, spotted by keywords, so look over the list. Everything goes to your own Trash, and Undo puts it back while it is still there.

                Your email accounts

                Gmail, Yahoo and iCloud use an app password: a separate password just for Sweepwise, so your real password is never shared. Outlook and Hotmail can't be connected yet; they will use Microsoft's own sign-in page.

                  About & privacy

                  Sweepwise

                  We collect nothing

                  No account, no tracking, no analytics, no crash reports. Your files and scans stay on this PC, except what the AI helper sends if you use it (see below).

                  Update checkOnce a day Sweepwise asks GitHub which version is newest. Only Sweepwise's own version number goes with the question; like any website, GitHub sees your internet address. You can switch it off in Settings.
                  AI helper (only if you add a key)When you use it, Claude or OpenAI (never the developer) gets names, sizes and dates of files and folders, Sweepwise's own results, your recent apps and folders, your notes and what you type; for email, senders and subject lines. Never what is inside your files. Names and subjects with private-looking words (like bank or tax) are left out.
                  Your recordsSweepwise keeps its history, settings and Undo records in its own folder on this PC. Open it any time.

                  • Nothing personal changes without your OK.
