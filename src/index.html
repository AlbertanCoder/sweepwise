Email

Clears old newsletters and promotions out of your inbox. Sweepwise reads only sender and subject lines, never your emails, and never touches mail from people you write to, banks, government, health, security alerts or receipts. Everything goes to your own Trash, and Undo puts it back.

Your email accounts

Gmail, Yahoo and iCloud use an app password: a separate password just for Sweepwise, so your real password is never shared. Outlook and Hotmail use Microsoft's own sign-in page.

Speed Boost

Make your PC feel new again with Microsoft's own tools: fewer programs starting with Windows, less junk, lighter settings on slow PCs and up-to-date apps. Every change waits for your OK, and Windows makes a restore point first.

Keep it like new

Weekly auto-cleanOff unless you switch it on. Every Sunday at 5 pm Sweepwise quietly clears temporary files and caches only: never your files, never moves anything. Each one shows in History.
Storage SenseWindows' own automatic clean-up. If you turn it on in Windows Settings, check it is not set to empty your Recycle Bin or Downloads unless you want that.
Driver updatesDrivers come only from Windows Update (optional updates). Sweepwise never uses third-party driver tools.
