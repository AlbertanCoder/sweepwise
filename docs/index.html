Three promises

Cleaners have a bad name for deleting things people needed. Sweepwise is built the other way round.

1

Nothing personal changes without your OK

After a first look, temporary files and caches are cleared straight away. Everything else waits on the Approve list until you press Approve, and you can untick anything first.

2

Changes you approve can be undone

Your own files only move or go to the Recycle Bin, and Sweepwise never empties it. History & Undo puts them back in one click. Uninstalls and app updates are the exceptions; temporary files, caches and Deep Clean's old system files are deleted for good.

3

Nothing is collected

No account, no tracking, no analytics, no crash reports. There are no Sweepwise servers at all.

What it does

One button for the safe stuff, then clear suggestions you approve. Windows maintenance uses Microsoft's own tools and settings (temporary files are cleared by Sweepwise itself): no registry cleaners, no driver updaters, no tricks.

Scan & Clean

Clears temporary files and caches, runs a quick virus scan with Windows Security, and gives your computer a health score with the reasons behind it.

  • The first time, it shows how much each kind of junk takes up and waits.
  • Tidies your Desktop and Downloads into folders, with your OK.
  • Finds leftovers from AI tools, programs you no longer use, and folders removed programs left behind.

Speed Boost

Helps a slow computer feel quicker: fewer programs starting by themselves, lighter settings on PCs with little memory, and up-to-date apps.

  • A before-and-after report shows the difference.
  • Before app updates and power or visual-effects changes, Windows makes a restore point. Every change except app updates has Undo.
  • An optional weekly auto-clean, off unless you turn it on.

Email clean-up (optional)

Connect Gmail, Yahoo or iCloud with an app password, a separate password just for Sweepwise. Outlook and Hotmail are coming.

  • Old bulk mail goes to your email's own Trash, with Undo.
  • Unsubscribe from real senders, in one click when the sender offers it.
  • Skips mail from people you've written to in the last year, and senders that look like banks, government, school or work (spotted by keywords, so look over the list).

AI helper, with your own key (optional)

Add a Claude or OpenAI key for smarter suggestions, the AI Organizer and a chat that answers questions about your computer. A built-in guide shows how to get a key in a few minutes.

  • The AI gets names, sizes and dates, Sweepwise's own results, email senders and subjects, and what you type, never what is inside your files.
  • It learns which apps and folders you use, and sends only a short list of them with your AI requests.
  • Most uses cost a few cents on your own account (a big AI Organizer plan can cost more), never on Sweepwise.

Security check

Protection, odd startup items, risky Chrome, Edge and Brave extensions, hijacked search or proxy settings and built-in safety switches.

Deep Clean

Windows' own Disk Cleanup for system files, plus Optimize Drives and update clean-up, with optional repair checks. Never empties your Recycle Bin.

Disk map & Space Finder

See what fills your drive, and find exact duplicates and big forgotten files.

History & Undo

Every approved change is written down the moment it happens, so one click puts it back (except uninstalls and app updates).

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes: every feature, for everyone, with no ads and no paid version. It's kept going by people who choose to support it, and supporting unlocks nothing extra.

Can it delete something I need?

Safe cleaning only touches temporary files and caches in a fixed list of places. Anything personal waits on the Approve list until you press Approve, your own files only move or go to the Recycle Bin, and History & Undo brings them back.

Does it replace my antivirus?

No. On Windows, Windows Security does the virus protection; Sweepwise checks it is switched on, runs its scans and looks for things that should not be there. On Linux it uses ClamAV, which it can install with one click.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

The download from GitHub isn't signed with a paid certificate yet, so Windows shows "Windows protected your PC". Click More info, then Run anyway.

Do I need an AI key?

No. Cleaning, security, speed, storage and email all work without one. A Claude or OpenAI key adds smarter suggestions, the AI Organizer and chat, and most uses cost a few cents on your own account.
