# Testing Sweepwise

Thank you for trying Sweepwise before it launches. Testing takes about 20 minutes on the first day, then a minute or
two whenever Sweepwise reminds you, for two to three weeks.

## Before you start

- You need Windows 10 or 11. (Linux and Mac testers will be asked separately later.)
- Sweepwise never deletes your own files for good: they only go to the Recycle Bin, and History & Undo puts them
  back. Temporary files and caches (junk that Windows and your apps rebuild) are deleted for good, after Sweepwise
  shows you the list the first time.
  Even so, this is a test version, so it's a good idea to have a backup of anything precious, as always.
- Nothing about you is collected or sent to the developer. A few things do go online, like the daily check for a new
  Sweepwise version and the app-update check during a scan; [PRIVACY.md](../PRIVACY.md) lists them all.

## Install

1. Open the test download link you were sent and save the file.
2. Open it. Because the test version isn't signed with a paid certificate yet, Windows may show
   **"Windows protected your PC"**. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. The installer shows the Sweepwise Test Terms. Read them and click **I Agree**; it won't install without your OK.
   (On Mac and Linux, Sweepwise shows the same terms the first time it opens.)
4. Sweepwise opens with a short welcome. Read the three promises, choose whether you want a weekly reminder, and skip
   the AI key unless you already have one.

## Day one: please try these

- [ ] **Scan & Clean.** The first time, Sweepwise shows what it would remove and waits. Does the list make sense? Press
      Clean now.
- [ ] **Approve list.** Look through the suggestions. Is anything suggested that you would never want touched? Tick a
      few safe ones and press Approve.
- [ ] **Undo.** Open History & Undo and undo that run. Did everything come back where it was?
- [ ] **Security.** Press Run Security check. Is anything confusing or alarming without a reason?
- [ ] **Speed Boost.** Press Find speed-ups, then Review speed-ups. Did the explanations make sense?
- [ ] **Disk map** and **Space Finder.** Do the big items match what you expected?
- [ ] **Help.** Did the Help page answer the questions you had?

Optional, only if you are comfortable:

- [ ] **Email:** connect a Gmail, Yahoo or iCloud account with an app password (the Email page shows how). Check the
      suggestions before approving anything; everything goes to your email's Trash, and Undo brings it back while it
      is still there (your provider may empty its Trash after 30 days).
- [ ] **AI helper:** if you already have a Claude or OpenAI key, add it in Settings and try the AI Organizer or Ask
      Sweepwise.

## What to report

Anything that surprised, confused or worried you counts, as well as anything that broke. Please include:

- what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead;
- a screenshot if you can (Windows key + Shift + S);
- your Windows version (Settings > System > About) and the Sweepwise version (bottom left of the window).

Reply to the message that invited you. Reporting on GitHub opens when Sweepwise goes public.

If Sweepwise shows an error, its own records folder (About & privacy > Open Sweepwise's own records) has a small file
of screen errors that can help find the problem. It never leaves your computer unless you choose to send it.

## When the test ends

Keep using Sweepwise if you like it; it will offer the public version when it's out. To remove it, first switch off
the weekly reminder and auto-clean in Sweepwise's Settings if you turned them on. Then uninstall it from Settings > Apps
like any other app, and tick "Delete the application data" to remove its records folder too.
